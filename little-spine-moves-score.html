How this works

Your Moves

You are not named Little Spine — that’s the kid in the book. You read his story, walk his pages, and want his ending finished. Announce the short name like an ability. Nathan adjudicates (Hope/Fear, Line, countdowns). These twist the story-space, not the Companions as enemies. The ✎ line is yours.

Reminder: Companions hold the Scroll of Spelling (1 use each per page). You hold this list + the chapbook. Want the ending. Walk toward danger. Fail helpfully.
Quote — speak the Legend
Say a nursery line out loud as if the book is law. For one beat, something in the scene obeys the moral — a door listens, a hazard hesitates, a station becomes obvious. It may also make the “helpful” danger more convincing. Nathan picks which.
✎ Make it yours — your favorite line to quote:
Bookmark — save a thing
Point at a person, place, object, or sentence and declare it bookmarked. This page, it can’t be fully erased, washed away, or forgotten without a bigger cost. Useful for seed-pages, stolen nouns, open doors, Companions mid-fall.
✎ Make it yours — how you mark it (press, stamp, whisper):
Tear — rip a strip free
Tear a usable strip from the scene — a margin, wallpaper, your cuff, a Warm-flaked ash ribbon. You get one paper-rope / bandage / white flag / bait-ribbon / thin bridge. It works once. It sounds awful. Perfect.
✎ Make it yours — what you usually tear from:
Fold — dog-ear the clock
Dog-ear the moment. Hitch one visible countdown by 1 — you choose Progress or Consequence; Nathan confirms. It sticks. Not a full stop; just a crease that buys a breath.

Your Moves (cont.)

Draw — sketch into place
Sketch something small into the scene: a handhold, a bait-sentence, a windbreak outline, a decoy Little Spine (the book kid), a glowing arrow to a station. It is paper-real — useful, brittle, and hilarious if someone breathes on it wrong.
✎ Make it yours — your signature doodle style:
Press — pin flat
Press a leaf, a page, or yourself hard against something. Pin a door shut, pin a Companion so the wind can’t lift them, flatten a hazard for a beat, or take a rubbing that copies one word onto your seed-page. Body-weight paper magic.
