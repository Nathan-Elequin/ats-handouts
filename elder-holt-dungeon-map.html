The loop (every room)

  • Wes reads the nursery page line out loud (excited).
  • Wes picks A / B / C / invents D — that becomes what the peril looks like.
  • You keep the same pillar underneath (heat / wet / hungry-text / tear / blank).
  • Party solves the pillar. Wes tries heroic assists and mostly fails. Threats prefer him.

What never changes

Room order. Pillar. Shatter Line math. Who the threat targets (him). Exit condition for that room.

CYOA is costume only. Dragon-stove or volcano picnic = still heat.

Geography

Outside: Elder Holt clearing · glowing stump-lectern chapbook.

Inside: five pages made spatial after the party steps through an illuminated page with him.

“Shatterglass” = broken woven light as place (prism / ozone / crack) — not a separate region.
