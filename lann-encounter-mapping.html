The Scattered Lann
Encounter Mapping

Twelve anchored experiences, scattered into the Feywild. Each one draws the world toward it — and must be reclaimed.

Ennalyn
played by Hannah
Heart Wrapped in Light
Experience: Lead Singer
expand ↓
A hand squeezing your heart, light streaming through clouds.
Matched Encounter
The Fey Rock Orchestra
The Lann's energy is pure performance — the thrill of being witnessed in music, the physical sensation of a song landing in someone's chest like a fist. Light streaming through clouds is a spotlight. This Lann has attached itself to the conductor's baton of an eternal fey orchestra making its way to perform for the Fairy Queen. The music it produces has become dangerously beautiful — fey nearby weep without knowing why. The orchestra refuses to part with the Lann: they've never sounded this good, and they know it. The encounter is a negotiation or contest of performance. The party must either out-perform the orchestra, offer a replacement instrument of equal emotional weight, or convince the conductor that the beauty they're carrying doesn't belong to them.
Darkened Sun
Experience: Loss of Heart
expand ↓
Still darkness before a storm, dead air and dark noon.
Matched Encounter
The Seelie Funeral Parade
An eclipse — a sun that has gone dark — is the most precise image for this Lann, and the Seelie Funeral Parade is the Feywild's grief made communal and contagious. The dead air and dark noon is the moment a procession passes your door. This Lann has become the "beloved deceased" at the center of the parade: all who encounter the procession are charmed into following and re-enacting their own greatest losses rather than the fey's. The parade grows longer with every traveler it absorbs. The party must honor the grief without being swallowed by it — breaking the charm requires completing a genuine act of mourning, not fighting their way through. The Lann will not release itself to anyone who hasn't truly lost something.
Mockingbird Calls
Experience: Brave Little Mockingbird
expand ↓
Reaching out in the dark, warmth, half-heard humming.
Matched Encounter
The Boggle Problem
Boggles are summoned into existence by loneliness and the craving for connection — which is precisely what "Brave Little Mockingbird" encodes: the courage to reach toward another person in the dark. The half-heard humming is the Lann's presence radiating from wherever it settled, drawing creatures toward it like a hearth light. A cluster of boggles has formed around an isolated fey creature — perhaps an old faun, a child abandoned at the threshold, a spirit that forgot its name — whose loneliness attracted them. The Lann is somewhere inside that tangle of dark-light and warmth. The encounter is about following the humming through genuine darkness, and the resolution is not combat but recognition: the party must identify and acknowledge the lonely heart at the center of the problem.
Drii
played by Ben
Walking With Ruin
Experience: Better Monster
expand ↓
An overwhelming heat, raging wildfire that consumes and seeks life to burn.
Matched Encounter
The Dryad's Conclave
Aria's militant dryad group is destruction born from grief — grief that became a wildfire. The Lann has amplified the Conclave's destructive power: the forest around them is hotter than it should be, fire spreads with unnatural eagerness, and the overgrowth chokes visibility to ten feet. The Lann didn't corrupt Aria — it just made her more of what she already was. The encounter asks the exact same moral question as Drii's experience: is this creature a monster, or is she someone who was made this way? Combat is possible but shallow. The deeper encounter is a negotiation with Aria, who will only speak honestly to someone who can demonstrate they also know what it is to walk with ruin — to have caused damage without meaning to, to be dangerous to things they love.
Ceaseless Spring
Experience: Silver-Tongued Faerie
expand ↓
A bubbling brook that smells slightly of brimstone and salt.
Matched Encounter
The Name-Stealing Fey
A brook that bubbles freshly but smells of brimstone is the sensory definition of the silver-tongued: appealing on every surface, subtly dangerous underneath. This Lann has come to rest at a crossing-stream where a particularly elegant name-stealing fey holds permanent court, using the brook's pleasant sound as cover. The Lann has made the fey's charm almost irresistible — every conversation with them feels like a gentle current pulling you downstream. The party will be three social contracts deep before they understand what's happening. The encounter is a game of asking the right question three times to force plain speech, while avoiding every polite trap ("may I have your name," "thank you for coming," "what can I offer you") along the way. Recovery of the Lann requires out-charming someone who has been doing this since before the continent had a name.
Woven Light Shattering
Experience: Made of Memory and Magic
expand ↓
A shifting kaleidoscopic mosaic in the blood, taste of electricity in the air, the distant crack of shifting ice.
Matched Encounter
The Shatterglass (annoying-escort dryad) · Wes
Ben's current Made of Memory and Magic slip (Fey name + sensory as written). The place must be the phrase: prismatic ice sheets underfoot (distant crack), ozone-static before every fissure (electricity on the tongue), and blood-warm veins of colored light crawling under the freeze (kaleidoscope mosaic). A frail adolescent dryad absorbed the Lann while reading a hero-legend; now he needs "brave adventurers" to escort him through a domain that literally shatters glass bones and paper leaf-skin. Gameplay is the classic annoying escort — he walks headlong into danger, rolls mostly fail / sometimes clutch, and wins by completing a rite that is secretly lame. Foil: Drii's blood already is that mosaic; the kid romanticizes the shatter as destiny.
Edmund
played by Dan
Wind Rustling Leaves in the Trees
Experience: Songwriter
expand ↓
Warm, familiar, new, just out of reach.
Matched Encounter
The Gossip Trees
The sensory description of this Lann is the Gossip Trees: the rustle of leaves as communication, warmth and familiarity in whispers, the feeling of something important being said just around the next bend. The Lann has nested into one great old tree, and every secret this tree shares now comes out as a half-remembered song — travelers leave the grove humming melodies they can't place. The tree has become the most sought-after gossip node in the forest, which means it's well-protected. To recover the Lann, the party must offer a secret that carries the same emotional weight as a song — something warm, something new, something that costs them. The tree isn't malicious; it just doesn't know the difference between a secret and a song, and neither does the Lann.
The Morning Dew
Experience: Quack
expand ↓
Care, confidence, smells of herbs and spices.
Matched Encounter
The Druid's Awakened Community
Morning dew and the smell of herbs and spices is a druid's grove at dawn. The "Quack" experience is Edmund's identity as a healer — care and confidence as a professional posture. This Lann's energy has been absorbed by one of the druid's awakened animals, who has become a practicing folk healer drawing every sick creature in the forest to a tiny woodland clinic. The awakened community has built itself around the Lann's influence. The political drama is already in motion: some animals don't want to give up their healer; the druid has lost control of what they created; and at least one awakened creature is secretly reporting to a hag who wants the Lann's healing energy for something much worse. Recovering the Lann means diagnosing the real illness in this community before treating it.
Sinking in Quicksand
Experience: Existential Crisis
expand ↓
The light going away, claustrophobic, pressure in the chest.
Matched Encounter
The Forest That Grows Behind You
The forest that grows when unobserved, consuming the geography behind you as you move forward — this is existential dread made into a biome. The light going away is the canopy closing. The claustrophobia is the forest tightening. The pressure in the chest is the moment you turn around and realize there is no path back. The Lann is at the heart of this haunt, and its proximity makes the forest more aggressive: trees grow faster, darkness falls sooner, the ground underfoot softens in ways that suggest depth. The Chimney Face-Stealer has moved in, feeding on the identity-void the Lann generates around it. The encounter's resolution isn't navigation — it's the willingness to name what you're afraid of becoming, out loud, in the dark, while the forest listens.
Kai
played by Ayla
Shifting Spirit
Experience: Be Whoever You Want Me to Be
expand ↓
A creature made of different breeds — rapidly shifting waters.
Matched Encounter
The Reflection Pool
This is the most direct match in the entire set. Rapidly shifting waters are the Reflection Pool's surface. A creature made of different breeds is what the Pool shows you when you look in — every version of yourself you could have been, all claiming equal reality. This Lann has been absorbed into the Pool and now generates endlessly shifting reflections of Kai specifically: every possible Kai, from every path not taken. The reflections move independently. Some of them wave. One of them doesn't. To recover the Lann, someone must enter the Pool and choose — not which reflection is real, but which one Kai is willing to let dissolve. The unchosen versions don't go quietly.
Made of Melody
Experience: Disciple of Song
expand ↓
Wind whistling through trees.
Matched Encounter
The Gravity Cliff
The Gravity Cliff is where gravity is determined by intention rather than physics — walk in genuine confidence and the ground holds; doubt yourself and you fall. For a Disciple of Song whose entire identity is devotion to music and to Lliira, this is a perfect structural mirror. The Lann has drifted to a wind-carved stone cliff face where the gaps in the rock produce natural music — not beautiful, not disturbing, just perpetually incomplete, as though waiting for the right listener to finish the phrase. Wind spirits have gathered around it, unable to leave, held by the feeling that the song is about to resolve. To cross the cliff and reach the Lann, someone must walk it with absolute conviction in their purpose — not performed conviction, not forced confidence, but the real thing. Kai's arc tension with Lliira makes this encounter particularly loaded.

Bonus Lann · Rent
Desire to Be Home with Kai

This one doesn't get an encounter. This one follows the party. A single small rain cloud, persistent and gentle, that appears somewhere in the first session after the scattering and simply... doesn't leave. It rains softly on the party's heads at inconvenient moments. It can be shielded against but never driven away. No one in the Feywild can tell them what it is — or they know, and they're not saying. It is not a threat. It is not a bargain. It is just homesick, in the only language it has.
